Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before partner applications open.

When does billing begin?

Billing begins only after your trial ends and you activate your subscription. Founder Partners receive a 90 day trial. Other approved salons receive a 14 day trial.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Partners can cancel their subscription. Access remains based on the active subscription and platform policy at that time.

Do customers pay through Elegentra?

No. Elegentra is a discovery platform. Customers browse, compare and contact beauty businesses directly.

What happens after my trial?

You can activate the Professional plan to keep partner tools and public listing access active after the trial period.

How does Founder Partner work?

The first 50 approved salons receive founder status, a 90 day trial, early feature access, priority support and a Founder Partner badge.
