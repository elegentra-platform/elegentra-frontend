Features

Everything needed to discover and present beauty businesses clearly.

Elegentra gives customers a calmer way to discover, compare and choose beauty and wellness businesses, and gives approved partners a premium place to be found.

Become a Founder PartnerView features

For customers

Simple discovery tools that help people decide with confidence before they contact a business.

01

Nearby beauty businesses

Find salons, spas, barber shops, nail studios, wellness businesses and beauty professionals by location and service intent without browsing through noise.

02

Rich salon profiles

See the details that matter: services, galleries, location, contact information, reviews where available and a clear business presentation.

03

Service galleries

Explore work, interiors and service photos in a clean visual gallery, then use profile details and reviews where available to choose with more confidence.

04

Map and directions

Move from discovery to visit planning quickly with location details and direction-ready profiles.

05

Direct contact

Reach businesses through simple contact actions instead of being pushed through a crowded checkout flow.

06

Verified businesses

Approved listings, useful profile information and reviews or ratings where supported help customers browse with more trust and less uncertainty.

For salon partners

Tools that help approved businesses look credible, stay current and understand customer interest.

01

Premium public profile

Show your business with a polished profile designed for mobile discovery, quick trust and easier customer comparison.

02

Gallery management

Keep your best work, interiors and service photos organized so customers can judge quality visually.

03

Service management

Present the services you offer clearly, so visitors understand what you are known for before they contact you.

04

Profile insights

Understand profile views, service interest, enquiry signals and customer discovery patterns as your listing grows.

05

Subscription management

Manage partner access with clear trial status, renewal details and secure billing support.

06

Founder benefits

Early approved partners receive stronger launch support, early access and a Founder Partner badge.

Built with simplicity

Discovery first. Marketplace noise last.

Elegentra is focused on helping customers find the right beauty business and contact them directly. The experience stays calm, visual and useful, helping customers compare profiles, services, galleries, locations and reviews without turning every decision into a crowded transaction flow.
